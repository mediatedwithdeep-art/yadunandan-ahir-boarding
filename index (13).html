About the boarding

A boarding, not a paying-guest room.

A hostel gives a student a bed. A boarding gives them a structure — fixed study hours, fixed meal times, a warden who notices when someone stops attending college, and rules that are the same for everyone.

Yadunanand Ahir Boarding was established by the community in Rajkot so that students from villages and smaller towns could study here without their families worrying about where they live, what they eat, or who they are with. That is still exactly what we do.

  • Run by શ્રી યદુનંદન આહીર કેળવણી મંડળ — the Ahir education society — not for profit
  • Resident warden on the premises at all times
  • Attendance and leave recorded — parents are informed
  • Walking / short-ride distance from major colleges in Rajkot

Why parents choose us

Six things we do not compromise on.

Every one of these is checkable. Visit unannounced and see for yourself — that offer is the point.

Security that is actually enforced

Entry register at the gate, CCTV on common areas and corridors, and a fixed in-time. Outsiders do not enter the residential floors.

Study hours are protected

Two fixed silent study sessions every day in a dedicated hall. Phones and noise stay out of it — that rule is the reason the hall works.

Pure vegetarian, home-style food

Fixed weekly menu, published in advance. Same kitchen, same food for everyone — and the mess is open for parents to inspect.

Cleanliness is a daily routine

Rooms, bathrooms and common areas cleaned every day on a written schedule. Water tanks and RO units serviced on record.

Discipline without harshness

Clear rules, told to the student on day one, applied equally. Warnings go to parents before anything escalates — no surprises.

Fees with nothing hidden

One published fee. Written receipt for every payment. What is included and what is not is listed openly in the fees section below.

Study environment

The reason a boarding beats a rented room.

A student living alone decides every night whether to study. Here, that decision is already made — the hall is open, everyone is in it, and the phone is not.

  • Two supervised study sessions daily — morning and night, in the study hall
  • Your own desk, not a shared table — a partitioned carrel per student, so nobody is studying in somebody else's line of sight
  • Silence is enforced — no phones, no conversation, no music in the hall
  • Extra hours during exams — the hall stays open longer through the exam period
  • Seniors help juniors — informally, and it is encouraged
  • Result review — policy on collecting results to be confirmed

Evening ground time.

A walled, floodlit ground inside the campus — so an hour outdoors does not mean an hour outside the gate.

Rules & discipline

We publish our rules before you apply, not after.

Read these with your son before applying. If any of them is a problem, this is not the right place — and it is better to know that now.

01

Fixed in-time

Every student must be inside the gate by the stated in-time. Late entry is recorded and reported to parents.

02

Leave requires written permission

Going home or staying out overnight requires prior approval and a parent's confirmation.

03

Study hours are compulsory

Attendance in both study sessions is mandatory. Repeated absence is treated as a disciplinary matter.

04

Zero tolerance: tobacco, alcohol, drugs

Possession or use of any intoxicant results in immediate removal from the boarding. No warning, no refund.

05

No outsiders on residential floors

Visitors, including friends, meet in the visitors' area only, during visiting hours.

06

Respect for staff and students

Ragging, bullying, casteist or abusive behaviour is a removal offence and will also be reported as required by law.

07

Care of property

Damage to rooms, furniture or common property is recoverable from the student concerned.

08

Cleanliness of your own space

Rooms are inspected. Students keep their bed, cupboard and desk in order; common areas are cleaned by staff.

09

Phones during study hours

Phones are not permitted inside the study hall during sessions. Full policy to be confirmed.

10

Attendance at college is monitored

Students found skipping college are reported to their parents the same week.

A signed copy of these rules is taken from both the student and the parent at the time of admission. Serious breaches are decided by the management committee, whose decision is final.

FAQ

The questions parents actually ask.

Can we visit before applying?
Yes, and we would prefer it. Come during working hours, see the rooms, the mess and the study hall, and talk to the warden. You do not need an appointment, though a call ahead helps us make sure someone is free to show you around.
Is the boarding only for students of one community?
This must be answered plainly and accurately by the management. Whatever the answer is, stating it clearly here saves families from a wasted journey.
How is my son's safety ensured?
A warden lives on the premises. There is a fixed in-time recorded in the gate register, CCTV coverage of entry points, corridors and common areas, and outsiders are not permitted on residential floors.
What if my son falls ill?
First aid is available on site and simple food is provided on request. For anything beyond that, we take the student to a doctor and inform parents immediately — not afterwards.
Can students go home on weekends?
Yes, with prior written permission and a parent's confirmation. Departure and return are entered in the register. Exact leave policy to be confirmed.
Are mobile phones allowed?
Phones are not permitted inside the study hall during study sessions. The full policy, including night hours, is to be confirmed.
Is the fee payable in instalments?
To be confirmed. Every payment, in any form, is issued against a written receipt.
What happens if a student breaks the rules?
Minor breaches get a warning, and parents are informed. Repeated breaches go to the management committee. Tobacco, alcohol, drugs and ragging result in immediate removal without refund — that is stated at admission and applied without exception.
How far is the boarding from the main colleges?
List the nearby colleges with actual distances or travel times. This is one of the strongest reasons a family chooses one boarding over another.
Is there a waiting list if seats are full?
Yes. Completed applications are queued in order of submission, and we call the moment a seat opens. Submitting early materially improves your chances.
