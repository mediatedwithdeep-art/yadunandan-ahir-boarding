## Two sections deliberately left empty

### Testimonials (`#voices`)
Built and styled, but showing no quotes. Invented testimonials are the fastest way to lose a
parent's trust — they check. For each real one you need: full name, relationship, course and
year, written permission, and a photo if they consent.

### Achievements (`#achievements`)
All four counters show `—`. Fill them from your own register and alumni records. Do not round
up: “147 students” is more credible than “1000+ happy students”.

## Contact details live in one place

In `index.html`, find the `SITE` object near the bottom and fill in four values:

```js
const SITE = {
  phone:    "+919876543210",      // office number, with country code
  whatsapp: "919876543210",       // digits only — no +, no spaces
  email:    "office@example.org",
  mapQuery: "Yadunanand Ahir Boarding, Rajkot, Gujarat"
};
```

That single edit activates every Call button, every WhatsApp link, the enquiry form and the map.
