Common questions

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, and we would prefer it. Come during working hours and see the rooms, mess and study hall. A call ahead helps us have someone free to show you around.

How is safety handled?

A warden is on the premises, there is a fixed in-time in the gate register, CCTV on corridors and the gate, extinguishers and marked fire exits on every floor.

What if a student falls ill?

First aid on site and simple food on request. Anything more and we take him to a doctor and call parents immediately.

Can students go home on weekends?

Yes, with prior written permission and a parent's confirmation. Exact leave policy to be confirmed.

What if seats are full?
